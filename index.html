Recap Paper
Recap paper
1
”Learning to Generate Reviews and Discovering Sentiment” is a research
paper authored by Alec Radford, Rafal Jozefowicz, and Ilya Sutskever.
2
The paper presents a novel approach to generating text reviews and
discovering sentiment in an unsupervised manner.
3
Unlike traditional sentiment analysis, which relies on labeled data, this work
leverages a large dataset to train a character-level recurrent neural network
(char-RNN) model.

Reproduce The Results
Reproduce The Results
First, we reproduce a model that processes text as a sequence of UTF-8 encoded
bytes, then it learns to predict the future bytes of the sequence.
Next, we train a logistic regression classiﬁer on top of our model for tasks
including semantic relatedness, text classiﬁcation, and paraphrase detection.
Model: 4096-d mLSTM, 64-d embedding, 256-d output.
Weight Norm: applied only to LSTM parameters (hidden to hidden/gate
weights), not embedding or output.
Optimizer : Adam.
Parameters : number of parameters: 87572736.
Learning Rate : 0.0005 per batch of 128. Linear Learning rate decays to 0
over the course of the epoch.
Gradient Clipping: clipped the gradients to a maximum of 1.
